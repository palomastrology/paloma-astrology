Her Journey

From Courtroom to Cosmos

My journey has been one of transformation, self-discovery and finding my true calling. I pursued my B.A. LL.B. from the Army Institute of Law, Mohali, and began my professional journey in the corporate sector, working with an insurance company.

Although I had built a promising career in law, somewhere within, I felt called towards something more meaningful — a profession where I could directly connect with people, understand their struggles and help them find clarity and direction. This is what led me towards Astrology, Face Reading and Healing.

“Sometimes your true calling lies beyond the career you originally chose.”

Today, through my work, I aim to help individuals understand themselves better, navigate life’s uncertainties and move forward with greater confidence and clarity. For me, astrology is not about creating fear or predicting a fixed destiny — it’s about understanding possibilities, recognising patterns and empowering people to make informed choices.
